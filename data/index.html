Data Access and Licence

All the bulk data published by Carbon & Place is available to download under the Open Data Commons Attribution License.

You are free:

  • To share: To copy, distribute, and use the data.
  • To create: To produce works from the data.
  • To adapt: To modify, transform, and build upon the data.

As long as you:

  • Attribute: You must attribute any public use of the data, or works produced from the data, in the manner specified in the licence. For any use or redistribution of the data, or works produced from it, you must make clear to others the licence of the data and keep intact any notices on the original data.

This licence does not apply to the website, map tiles, or other hosted data, which is only to be used via the official Carbon & Place website. You must not link, embed, scrape or otherwise use the constituent parts of the website.

Experimental Data Releases

As Carbon & Place is undergoing a major update, many of the new tools are in an experimental stage. Experimental data releases are a bulk version of the data available in the Carbon & Place. However, these should be treated with care as they may contain errors. Once tools have been finalised, experimental data releases will be replaced with bulk data downloads.

Experimental datasets are zipped and named by date of creation (e.g. yyyymmdd).

2 m Digital Terrain Model of Great Britain

A PMtiles file containing RGB encoded webp raster tiles. A DTM is a raster map containing the elevation of the ground. This version was constructed to be an Open Data terrain map with a higher resolution than commonly available sources like OS Terrain 50. For more details on methods and data see the GitHub repo.
