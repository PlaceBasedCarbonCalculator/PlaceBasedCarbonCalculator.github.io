How to use the site

Three steps for anyone new to Carbon & Place.

1

Find your area

Enter a postcode above for a combined local report, or open a tool and click any neighbourhood on the map.

2

Explore the tools

Each tool covers a different subject. Colour the map by any measure and open the detailed reports.

3

Compare and share

Compare your neighbourhood with its authority, ward and constituency, then share any view by copying the web address.
