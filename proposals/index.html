Homepage design proposals

Three directions for refreshing the Carbon & Place homepage. Every one keeps the parallax hero you like and the brand colours, and reworks everything below it for clearer structure and better mobile behaviour.

These are drafts for review only. The live index.html is untouched. Open each on desktop and narrow your browser (or use device emulation) to see the responsive layout. Links inside the mock-ups point at the real tools.
Proposal 1
Clean & scannable

Tool card deck

A responsive grid of tool cards with a coloured accent, screenshot, short blurb and clear call to action. Postcode search floats over the hero. Least text, most scannable.

Proposal 2
Warm & narrative

Editorial feature rows

Full-width alternating feature rows with generous typography and a coloured kicker per tool. Reads like a modern research site and gives each tool room to breathe.

View proposal 2 →
Proposal 3
Action first

Postcode-first dashboard

Puts the postcode search front and centre as the hero action, with a compact tile grid of tools below. Sleek, utilitarian, light and dark aware. Gets people into a tool fastest.
