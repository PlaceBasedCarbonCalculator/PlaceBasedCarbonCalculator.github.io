Carbon footprints · Great Britain

Place-Based Carbon Calculator v2 beta

Beta tool: results may include preliminary analysis or incomplete features. Please report issues on GitHub.

Estimates the carbon footprint of every neighbourhood in Great Britain, breaks it down into housing, transport and consumption, and tracks how it has changed since 2010.

Click any neighbourhood on the map to open its report, or use the layer controls to change what the colours show. The help buttons explain each map and chart.
